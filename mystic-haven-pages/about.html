About Me & Mystic Haven

About Me

Matt Marais — The Guided Witch

I'm Matt Marais, known online as The Guided Witch. I've been fascinated by magic, mythology and the occult for as long as I can remember — but the real turning point came at my grandparents' house, sitting at an old computer, when I searched "real life magic" and a video on witchcraft popped up. It was like a door opened inside me, and it's never closed since. I've been learning and practising ever since I was 11 or 12, and in 2024 I began practising professionally. I'm an eclectic witch, working mostly with elemental magic, spirit work, baneful magic and justice work. I'm deeply empathetic, and that's at the heart of everything I do: my dream is to use my platform to spread light, love, guidance and hope. I believe I'm here to be seen, to teach, and to help — and I'm working toward funding three organisations for the less fortunate and for animals.

About Us

The Vision of Mystic Haven

Mystic Haven was founded on 25 July 2024 with one purpose: to help people and share my gift. It has become my most cherished creation and goal, and it's allowed me to become ten times what I thought I could be — including the income I used to only dream of. Mystic Haven is run by me, Matt Marais (The Guided Witch), together with my team. We're a South African business striving to become the biggest occult and spirituality store in the country, and we're planning big things for her. Mystic Haven is for everyone — a place of peace, healing and justice, something I never had. And this is only the beginning.
